## 3. Huong dan tung buoc giai quyet va toi uu hoa quy trinh

De xu ly nhanh chong bai toan doc tai lieu 150 trang va go bo cac diem nghen trong xu ly du lieu, ban hay thuc hien theo quy trinh chuan duoi day.

### Buoc 1: Chuan bi va trich xuat noi dung tu file PDF phap ly
Truoc tien, hay dam bao rang ban dang su dung phien ban Claude 3.5 Sonnet moi nhat. Tai lieu PDF cua ban can duoc kiem tra so bo de tranh loi font chu.

* Mo giao dien Claude 3.5 Sonnet tren trinh duyet.
* Tai len file PDF phap ly 150 trang bang cach nhan vao bieu tuong dinh kem tep (file attachment).

### Buoc 2: Su dung Prompt chuyen sau de tom gon trong 3 phut
Thay vi dat nhung cau hoi chung chung, hay su dung cau lenh (prompt) co cau truc ro rang de AI trich xuat dung trong tam ma ban dang can cho ban thiet ke.

```text
Ban la mot chuyen gia tu van phap ly doanh nghiep. Hay phan tich tap tai lieu PDF 150 trang nay va cung cap cho toi:
1. Tom tat 5 diem thay đổi quan trọng nhat anh huong truc tiep den hoat dong cua cong ty.

2. Danh sach cac rui ro tuan thu kem theo muc do nghiem trọng (Cao, Trung binh, Thap).

3. Mot bang tom tat cac chi so, thoi han va muc phat lien quan de dua vao phu luc ban thao.
Yeu cau: Trinh bay ngan gon, dung trong tam, su dung bullet points ro rang.
```

### Buoc 3: Xu ly nhanh su co khoa sheet Excel cua dong nghiep
Trong truong hop file Excel bi khoa mat khau ma khong the mo duoc bang cach thong thuong va can chinh sua ngay lap tuc de dua vao bao cao, ban co the ap dung meo nho nay thong qua tinh nang ma hoa hoac xu ly nhanh bang VBA co ban neu duoc phep:

* Nhan **Alt + F11** trong Excel de mo giao dien VBA.
* Chon **Insert > Module** va dan doan ma xoa mat khau tam thoi cho sheet hien tai:

```vba
Sub UnprotectSheet()
    Dim i As Integer, j As Integer, k As Integer
    Dim l As Integer, m As Integer, n As Integer
    Dim i1 As Integer, i2 As Integer, i3 As Integer
    Dim i4 As Integer, i5 As Integer, i6 As Integer
    On Error Resume Next
    For i = 65 To 66: For j = 65 To 66: For k = 65 To 66
    For l = 65 To 66: For m = 65 To 66: For i1 = 65 To 66
    For i2 = 65 To 66: For i3 = 65 To 66: For i4 = 65 To 66
    For i5 = 65 To 66: For i6 = 65 To 66
    ActiveSheet.Unprotect Chr(i) & Chr(j) & Chr(k) & _
    Chr(l) & Chr(m) & Chr(i1) & Chr(i2) & Chr(i3) & _
    Chr(i4) & Chr(i5) & Chr(i6)
    If ActiveSheet.ProtectContents = False Then
        MsgBox "Trang tinh da duoc mo khoa thanh cong!"
        Exit Sub
    End If
    Next: Next: Next: Next: Next: Next
    Next: Next: Next: Next: Next: Next
End Sub
```

* Nhan **F5** de chay doan ma va hoan tat viec mo khoa vung du lieu dang bi chan.

## 4. Loi khuyen va canh bao tu chuyen gia

Khi ap dung cac giai phap tu dong hoa va AI vao cong viec hang ngay tai van phong, ban can luu y nhung diem sau de dam bao an toan thong tin:

* **Bao mat du lieu nhay cảm:** Khong tai len cac tai lieu chua thong tin mat cua khach hang hoac du lieu tai chinh noi bo qua cac kenh AI cong khai neu chua co su cho phep tu bo phan phap ly hoac bao mat thong tin (IT Security).
* **Kiem tra cheo thong tin:** Du Claude 3.5 Sonnet co toc do xu ly cuc ky an tuong, ban van nen doi chieu lai cac dieu khoan phap ly quan trong voi van ban goc de dam bảo tinh chinh xac tuyet doi truoc khi trinh lanh dao.
* **Xay dung thu vien prompt chuan:** Luu tru lai cac cau lenh hieu qua nhat vao mot file ghi chu chung de ca phong ban co the tai su dung trong cac du an tiep theo.
